Purchases

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the same App Store or Google Play account used for the purchase. Purchases are managed by the store; cross-store transfers are not provided.

Voyages and local records

The timer uses the saved departure time and voyage plan. Notification delivery can depend on device permissions and system settings. Your logbook and ship preferences are stored on your device; uninstalling the app may remove local records.

Ads

Ads are optional and appear only after choosing an ad-supported trip. Availability can vary. Settings shows Ad privacy options when required for your region.
