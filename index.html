NaviStella

A pixel-art space voyage for your focus time.

Choose a destination, set a focus and maintenance rhythm, and travel through space while you work. Your completed focus time stays in your logbook.

Customize your ship with free body colors and wings. Optional one-time purchases unlock destinations, flame colors, and three themed ships: a train, a UFO, and a jellyfish. A rewarded ad can provide one trip to a locked destination when an ad is available.
